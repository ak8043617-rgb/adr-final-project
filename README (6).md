# Final Project: Individual Case Safety Report (ICSR)
**Role:** Drug Safety Associate

## 1. Case summary
A 45-year-old female started **amoxicillin**. About 3 days later she developed a **skin rash and itching**, with **nausea and dizziness**. She also takes **paracetamol as needed**. Amoxicillin was stopped and the patient **recovered**.

## 2. Case validity check (minimum ICSR criteria)
| Criterion | Present? | Detail |
|-----------|----------|--------|
| Identifiable patient | Yes | 45-year-old female |
| Identifiable reporter | Yes | [reporter name / designation] |
| Suspect drug | Yes | Amoxicillin |
| Adverse event | Yes | Rash, itching, nausea, dizziness |

All four minimum criteria are met, so this is a **valid ICSR** and must be processed.

## 3. Deliverable 1: ADR identification
- **Timeline:** amoxicillin started (Day -5), symptoms began (Day -2, about 3 days after the first dose), patient presented (Day 0).
- **Suspected drug:** amoxicillin. Paracetamol is a concomitant drug with low suspicion.
- **Conclusion:** this is an Adverse Drug Reaction. The reaction began after the drug was started, and rash and itching are known reactions to penicillins.
- **Type:** Type B (hypersensitivity). The nausea may be a dose-related Type A effect.

### F. Case narrative
A 45-year-old female started amoxicillin, with paracetamol taken as needed. About 3 days after starting, she developed a skin rash and itching, with nausea and dizziness. Amoxicillin was withdrawn and the patient recovered after stopping the drug. Rechallenge was not performed. No other cause for the reaction was identified. The event is assessed as probably related to amoxicillin.

## 5. Deliverable 3: Causality assessment

**Naranjo algorithm**
| # | Question | Answer | Score |
|---|----------|--------|-------|
| 1 | Previous conclusive reports? | Yes | +1 |
| 2 | Event after the drug was given? | Yes | +2 |
| 3 | Improved on stopping the drug? | Yes | +1 |
| 4 | Reappeared on rechallenge? | Not done | 0 |
| 5 | Alternative causes? | No | +2 |
| 6-10 | Placebo, drug level, dose-response, previous reaction, objective evidence | Not known | 0 |
| | **Total** | | **6 = Probable** |

**WHO-UMC scale:** **Probable / Likely.** The time relationship is plausible, no other cause is found, and the response to withdrawal is reasonable. It is not Certain because rechallenge was not done.

## 6. Deliverable 4: Classification

| Parameter | Classification | Reason |
|-----------|----------------|--------|
| **Seriousness** | **Non-serious** | No death, life-threat, hospitalization, disability or congenital anomaly. The patient recovered. |
| **Expectedness** | **Expected (listed)** | Rash, pruritus and nausea are listed reactions in amoxicillin's product information. |
| **Causality** | **Probable** | Naranjo 6 and WHO-UMC Probable. |
| **Outcome** | **Recovered** | Resolved after drug withdrawal. |

Reporting category: non-serious, expected, spontaneous report. It is submitted through routine reporting, not as an expedited report.

## 7. Case processing steps followed
1. Receive the case and check the four minimum criteria.
2. Enter the data and code the reaction terms with MedDRA.
3. Assess seriousness, expectedness and causality.
4. Write the narrative and quality-check the case.
5. Submit the ICSR to the national pharmacovigilance centre (PvPI / CDSCO in India) and follow up if needed.

## 8. Recommendation
Record amoxicillin as a suspected cause of rash in the patient's file, and avoid re-exposure to amoxicillin and related penicillins unless a doctor advises otherwise. Report the case to the pharmacovigilance centre.
